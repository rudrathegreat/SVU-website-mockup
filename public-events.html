Featured Experience

Science and the Stars

Travel from nearby planets to the dynamic structure of the Milky Way, guided by experts and surrounded by cinematic scale.

The flagship experience combines astrophysical data, spatial audio and real-time visualisation. It is designed to make black holes, planets, moons and the structure of our galaxy feel immediate, dimensional and alive.

Format
Expert-guided immersive experience
Setting
45 megapixel stereoscopic 3D display
Location
Swinburne University, Hawthorn

Inside the Experience

A guided flight through real science.

Every session is shaped by a presenter who can move through the virtual environment in real time, changing scale and perspective as the story unfolds.
