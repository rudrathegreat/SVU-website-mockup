100m2, 45 megapixel LED wall

The Swinburne Virtual Universe is a 45 megapixel 120Hz custom-built room, delivering compelling 3D imagery and interactive animations.

360-degree spatial audio system

The Swinburne Virtual Universe offers an immersive 360-degree spatial audio system.

Real scientific data

The Swinburne Virtual Universe utilises real scientific data from NASA, the European Space Agency, and Swinburne's supercomputer infrastructure to help visitors learn about the universe.
